How the setup works

01

We understand your business

What you sell, how customers order, how you receive payment, how you deliver, and what you currently struggle to track.

02

We build your customer-facing system

A clean landing page or storefront that explains your offer, shows your products or services, and sends customers into a structured order flow.

03

We build your backend tracker

A dashboard using Google Sheets, Airtable, Notion, or a custom database later. This is where sales, expenses, customers, and balances are recorded.

04

You start seeing your numbers clearly

Every week, you can see what came in, what went out, who owes, what sold, and what needs to improve.
