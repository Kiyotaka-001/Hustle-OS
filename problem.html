The problem

Nigerian businesses are selling every day, but many are still bleeding money.

Sales alone do not mean profit. A business can make ₦500k in a month and still be broke because delivery fees, debt, returns, stock mistakes, ads, discounts, and personal spending are not being tracked properly.

01

WhatsApp orders are scattered

Customers send messages, voice notes, screenshots, and payment proofs in different chats. The business owner has no clean order history, no customer record, and no simple way to know who ordered what.

02

No proper money tracking

Money enters the account, but the business does not separate sales, delivery, stock cost, expenses, debt, profit, and owner withdrawals. So the business looks active but feels broke.

03

No customer database

Many businesses do not know their best customers, repeat buyers, inactive buyers, or people they should follow up with. This makes repeat sales harder.

04

No invoice or receipt structure

A serious customer wants clarity. Without invoices, receipts, and payment records, the business looks less professional and becomes harder to trust.

05

No inventory visibility

Restaurants lose money through bad stock control. Vendors forget what is available. Products sell out without being recorded. Small losses build up silently.

06

No weekly business report

Without a simple weekly summary, the owner cannot see what sold, what failed, what money is pending, what customers need attention, and what to improve next.
