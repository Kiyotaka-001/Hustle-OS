Features

Everything your business needs to stop guessing.

Hustle.os is built to give small businesses practical tools they can use immediately, without needing a complicated app or full SaaS platform from day one.

Storefront Invoices Orders Customers Expenses Profit Inventory Reports Storefront Invoices Orders Customers Expenses Profit Inventory Reports

Simple Website / Storefront

A clean page that explains what the business sells, shows products or services, builds trust, and directs customers to order.

WhatsApp Ordering System

Customers can click a button and send a structured order message with the right details, instead of sending incomplete random messages.

Invoice and Receipt Generator

The business can send professional invoices and receipts for orders, balances, deposits, and completed payments.

Sales Tracker

Track total sales, daily sales, best-selling items, pending payments, discounts, refunds, and customer purchases.

Expense Tracker

Track stock costs, delivery, ads, packaging, staff, rent, tools, and other business spending that affects profit.

Customer Database

Store customer names, numbers, order history, preferences, birthdays, balances, referrals, and follow-up notes.

Debt / Credit Tracker

Know who has paid, who owes, how much is pending, and when follow-up is needed. Perfect for vendors, photographers, event vendors, and service providers.

Inventory Tracker

See what is in stock, what is running low, what sold fast, and what needs to be restocked before money disappears.

Weekly Business Report

A simple weekly summary showing sales, expenses, profit estimate, pending payments, top products, and key next actions.
